// About AltSake

Whowe are

An independent label built on one simple idea: artists go further when they have people around them who actually care.

Our story

AltSake Records was started by an independent artist who knows how hard it is to do everything alone: the writing, the production, the visuals, the promotion and the business.

So we built the thing we wished existed. A label where artists, producers, engineers, digital artists and more work side by side, share what they know and push each other forward.

We're not a faceless company and we never want to be. We work like a family. That means looking out for each other, being honest, celebrating each other's wins and showing up when someone needs a hand.

Our goal

Our goal is simple: to help every artist on AltSake reach their goal.

Whether that's your first release, your first thousand listeners, a sold-out show or making music your full-time job, we want to help you get there. You stay you. We help you get there faster.

If you help the others,
you help yourself. — The AltSake way

How we grow together

Let's be real: our budget is small right now. We'd rather say that up front than promise things we can't deliver.

But here's the plan. Say one artist on the label goes mega viral. Suddenly the label has money, and that money doesn't disappear into one pocket. It goes back into everyone signed:

  • Studio sessions
  • Production
  • Photoshoots
  • Media coverage
  • Music videos
  • And more

That's why it matters so much that we stay friendly and help each other. Every stream you send to a labelmate, every mix you help with and every idea you share makes the whole family stronger, including you.

// What we stand for

Our values

Real people

We sign humans with something to say. Your story, your sound and your personality are the point.

Hard work

Talent gets you noticed. Work keeps you going. We back people who put in the hours, and we put them in with you.

Family first

No egos, no backstabbing. We support each other, give honest feedback and celebrate every win together.

Zero AI music

We're against using AI to create music. Every song we put out is made by real people. AI submissions are blocked permanently.
