What to include

  1. A short introductionWho you are, where you're from, what you make and what you're aiming for.
  2. A link to your musicReleased or unreleased. Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, or a private Google Drive or Dropbox link all work. Please make sure the link works.
  3. Your socialsIf you have any, add them. No following? No problem. It's not a requirement.

We use this to judge whether we're the right fit for each other. If it's a match, we'll reach out by email.

warning.sys

⚠ Zero-tolerance AI policy

AltSake is against using AI to create music. Any submission containing AI-generated music, including AI vocals, instrumentals, lyrics or full songs, will be blocked.

Anyone who submits AI music will never get a chance with us again. We see it as unserious, and we only work with real people who put in real work.
